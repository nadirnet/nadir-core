SHADE

A hand-shadow puppet theater. Your hands cast the level.

No headset detected. Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser for the hands-only experience, or try it with a mouse:

Sit comfortably. A lantern rests in your lap and a paper screen floats ahead.
Raise your hands into the light. Their shadows are solid in Kit's world: make bridges, elevators, walls and umbrellas.
Closer to the lantern = bigger shadow. Hold a shadow over a card to choose it. Tap the wooden tokens on the tray to restart, return to the menu, slow the game down, or recenter.
No controllers needed. Every interaction fits within arm's reach.
Mouse moves your hand's shadow · Wheel: nearer/farther from lantern · 1–6: hand shapes · W: rotate 90° · Q/E: tilt · R: restart · M: menu · S: slow mode
